Wrap-up

You just did entrepreneurial marketing.

Segment → Target → Position. You chose a traveller type. The AI matched it against a specific, provable position.
Brand. One name, one address, one phone everywhere, so the AI knew all the reviews were about the same Ruka.
4Ps. You turned the product into facts, made the price clear and fixed place (how guests book). Fact-based promotion is what AI repeats.
Sales process. The AI did the prospecting, qualifying and presenting for Ruka. The guest who clicks through is already pre-qualified.

Teaching simulation. Ruka Maldives, Stone Hotels Dhiffushi, Reveries Diving Village and Arena Beach Hotel are real Maldivian properties, described only with publicly listed facts. The "before" problems are a teaching scenario, and the "AI" and its rankings are scripted to show how AI shortlisting works, informed by Morand & Schegg (2026), Be Visible to AI, HES-SO Valais-Wallis.
