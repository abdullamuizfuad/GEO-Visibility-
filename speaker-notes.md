## Facilitating questions (they score this)
1. **Ask, pause, redirect:** "Good question. Before I answer, what do the rest of you think?"
2. **Build:** "Yes, and adding to that…"
3. **Link it back to the case:** "So for Ruka, that would mean…"
4. **Challenge with the textbook:** "Can a 12-room guesthouse serve three segments? What would Barringer and Ireland say?" (Answer: one core brand, segment-specific proof points; don't chase everyone.)

Use it once or twice. Answer a direct question from the panel chair directly.

## If the panel asks about the source
"Morand & Schegg's 2026 white paper, from HES-SO Valais-Wallis, is practitioner research, not peer-reviewed, and it was written for European hotels. I'm adapting its ideas to Maldivian guesthouses. The evidence is promising but early, and I'd teach it that way."
